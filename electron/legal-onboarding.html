End-user licence and operating terms

1. Conditional licence. All In One POS is licensed as a month-to-month service for the registered shop and authorised terminals. It is not sold as a transfer of the underlying software.

2. Account and terminal access. The shop owner is responsible for authorised users, terminal seats, passwords, activation tokens, and activity performed through the account. Do not share credentials or activation tokens with unauthorised people.

3. No tampering or unauthorised copying. Do not modify the Electron container, bypass access controls, inspect or extract secrets, or copy a terminal configuration to an unauthorised computer. The service may lock a terminal when its hardware identity, activation state, billing state, or lease is invalid.

4. Connectivity and offline operation. The POS may queue sales locally during a temporary outage. Queued sales remain subject to later server validation and synchronisation. A terminal lease can expire when it has not successfully checked in within the configured period.

5. Time and security checks. The terminal records a local last-seen time and may lock when the computer clock moves backwards or when other integrity checks fail. Correct the computer time and reconnect the terminal; do not attempt to bypass a lock.

6. Payments and records. Cashiers must only record a card payment after the physical Yoco, iKhokha, or other configured terminal shows approval. PayFast is used for the configured subscription billing flow. The POS does not store full card details. The shop remains responsible for cash-up, stock counts, pricing, tax, licensing, and legal compliance.

7. Data responsibility. The system provides operational tools, audit records, backups/synchronisation where available, and shop-scoped access controls. Review records regularly and report suspected unauthorised access promptly. This agreement is an operational software notice, not legal, tax, accounting, or financial advice.

8. Changes and support. The service provider may update the software, security controls, supported integrations, and terms. Contact the authorised administrator for billing, terminal replacement, or access problems.
